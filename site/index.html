Enter an address to see its assigned schools and how nearby options compare. Coverage: Washington and Multnomah counties, Oregon.
Or browse a school's catchment (no address)
Growth and on-track metrics reflect the school; proficiency levels and neighborhood outcomes reflect who enrolls and are shown as context, not a school score. Private schools may show blanks where comparable public data does not exist; a blank is missing data, not a low score.
Static demo: data is baked at build time. Addresses are geocoded by the U.S. Census geocoder; drive times use the public OSRM demo server and fall back to labeled straight-line distance.
